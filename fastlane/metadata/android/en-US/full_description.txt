Pterodactyl Panel is an Android app to manage your self-hosted Pterodactyl Panel instance.

Manage your own servers:
You can manage all the aspects of your servers: Console, Files, Databases, Schedules, Subusers/Users, Backups, Network/Allocations, Startup, Settings and view its Activity.

Manage your own User:
You can change your email or password, toggle 2FA on or off, manage API and SSH keys and view your own activity.

Admin Only Features:

Manage others' servers:
Manage other people's servers the same way you manage your own.

Manage the locations:
View, create, update and delete the panel's locations.

Manage the nodes:
View, create, update and delete the panel's nodes along with their allocations.

Manage the servers:
View, create, update and delete the panel's servers ("Databases" tab is read-only and "Mounts" tab is missing as per API limitations).

Manage the users:
View, create, update and delete the panel's users.

Preview the nests & their eggs:
View the panel's nests & their eggs (NOTE: read-only as per API limitations)

NOTE: Application settings, API, Databases, and Mounts are not available because there is no API endpoint to retrieve or manage them.
